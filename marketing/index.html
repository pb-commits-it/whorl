Cited, not hallucinated

Every claim resolves to a paragraph in an LLM-curated wiki built from KSU extension, IRAC, FRAC, HRAC, EPA PPLS, Cornell biocontrol, and ATTRA.

Resistance-aware

Reads your last-90-day spray history. Forces a different MOA group from the most recent same-pest application. Always surfaces a non-chemical alternative.

Built in public

AGPL-3.0. Self-hostable on a $14/mo VPS. v0.1 → v1.0 staged release log on GitHub. Issues + PRs welcome.

How it works

  1. Sign in with a magic link (no password). Add your farms and fields.
  2. Walk a field. Drop phone photos into the dropzone. Pest IDs stream in over a few seconds via SSE.
  3. The recommender pulls your last 90 d of applications, the local 7-day spray window, and the top-5 wiki chunks per pest × crop × region, then runs Qwen3-VL with strict-JSON output enforcing MOA rotation + REI/PHI citation.
  4. You get one plain-English sentence a farmer can act on today, plus the structured fields a consultant would write up.
