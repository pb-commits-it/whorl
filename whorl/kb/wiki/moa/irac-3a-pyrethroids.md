# IRAC 3A — pyrethroids

Voltage-gated sodium-channel modulators. Type-I pyrethroids (bifenthrin,
permethrin) and Type-II pyrethroids (lambda-cyhalothrin, cypermethrin)
both fall in IRAC 3A — **they share cross-resistance**, so rotating
between them does *not* satisfy a rotation requirement
`[irac-moa-v11.1]`.

## Representative active ingredients

- [[products/bifenthrin]]
- lambda-cyhalothrin
- permethrin
- cypermethrin

## Resistance footprint

Widespread documented resistance in *Helicoverpa zea*, *Helicoverpa
armigera*, *Spodoptera frugiperda*, and *Plutella xylostella*
populations globally. In the US Southern Plains and Mid-South,
*H. zea* tolerance is documented and field failures are increasingly
common.

## Rotation strategy

A 3A application must be followed by a chemistry from a **different
target site**, such as [[moa/irac-5-spinosyns]] or
[[moa/irac-28-diamides]]. **Do not** rotate within sub-groups (Type-I
↔ Type-II) — same MOA, same resistance mechanisms.
