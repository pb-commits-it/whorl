# IRAC 28 — diamides

Ryanodine receptor modulators — bind the calcium-release channel in
insect muscle, causing uncontrolled calcium release, feeding cessation,
paralysis, and death `[irac-moa-v11.1]`.

## Representative active ingredients

- [[products/chlorantraniliprole]]
- cyantraniliprole
- flubendiamide
- tetraniliprole

These share cross-resistance — rotation among them does not satisfy a
rotation requirement.

## Strengths

- Best-in-class residual on Lepidoptera (14–21 days under Kansas
  summer conditions)
- Selective for beneficial arthropods
- Rapid feeding cessation
- Active by ovicidal + larvicidal action when applied at early egg lay

## Resistance footprint

Documented in *Plutella xylostella* (diamondback moth) populations in
Asia. Maintain rotation discipline to delay resistance evolution in
Lepidoptera populations including *Helicoverpa zea*.

## Rotation strategy

Rotate with [[moa/irac-5-spinosyns]] (no cross-resistance) and/or
[[moa/irac-3a-pyrethroids]] where 3A susceptibility is maintained.
