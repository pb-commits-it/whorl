# IRAC 5 — spinosyns

Nicotinic acetylcholine receptor allosteric modulators binding at a
distinct site (Site I) from IRAC 4 neonicotinoids `[irac-moa-v11.1]`.
Fermentation-derived natural products with strong selectivity profiles.

## Representative active ingredients

- [[products/spinosad]]
- spinetoram

Spinosad and spinetoram share cross-resistance — rotation between them
does not count as rotation.

## Strengths

- Highly active on Lepidoptera (corn earworm, armyworms, leafrollers)
- Selective: low toxicity to most beneficial arthropods
- Active at low rates
- Rapid mode of action

## Resistance footprint

Limited compared to pyrethroids, but documented in *Plutella xylostella*
(diamondback moth) and some thrips populations. Maintain MOA rotation
to preserve.

## Rotation strategy

Excellent rotation partner with [[moa/irac-3a-pyrethroids]] (where
susceptibility is maintained) and [[moa/irac-28-diamides]] — both
different target sites.
