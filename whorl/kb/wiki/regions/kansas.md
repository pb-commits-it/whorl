# Regional notes — Kansas

Kansas row-crop pest pressure splits roughly along I-70:

| Band | Major early-season pests | Major ear-stage / reproductive pests |
|---|---|---|
| **North of I-70** | European corn borer (1st gen.), wireworm, southern corn rootworm | Western bean cutworm, second-gen ECB, *H. zea* on later silking |
| **South of I-70** | Fall armyworm, sugarcane aphid (on sorghum) | *H. zea*, fall armyworm, sorghum headworm complex |

## Pyrethroid resistance footprint

*H. zea* tolerance to IRAC 3A pyrethroids is documented across the
Southern Plains including southern Kansas. Default to
[[moa/irac-5-spinosyns]] or [[moa/irac-28-diamides]] for *H. zea*
unless a recent susceptibility bioassay supports 3A use.

## Extension references

KSU Department of Entomology publishes annually updated insect
management guides: MF742 (soybeans), MF743 (corn), MF745 (sorghum),
MF809 (wheat).
