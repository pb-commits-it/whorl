# Corn — *Zea mays* (field corn)

Dominant row crop across the Midwest. Most insect-pest pressure splits by
growth stage: pre-tassel feeders attack the **whorl** (corn whorl =
funnel-shaped center of unfolded leaves), and ear-stage feeders attack
silks and kernels during **silking → milk → dough**.

## Growth-stage cheat-sheet for scouting

| Stage | Whorl pests | Ear pests |
|---|---|---|
| V4–V8 (whorl) | [[pests/spodoptera-frugiperda]], [[pests/ostrinia-nubilalis]] (1st gen.) | — |
| VT–R1 (tassel → silk) | — | [[pests/helicoverpa-zea]], second-gen ECB |
| R2–R4 (blister → dough) | — | *H. zea* late instars on kernels |

## Bt-trait coverage notes

When the planted hybrid carries pyramided Bt traits (e.g. Cry1A.105 +
Cry2Ab2 + Vip3A), defaulting to **monitor** rather than *treat* is
appropriate unless the documented threshold for the relevant pest is
clearly breached — the trait stack handles routine pressure. Always
confirm refuge compliance before deferring to the trait `[ksu-mf743, p.13]`.
