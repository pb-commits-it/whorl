# *Bacillus thuringiensis* var. *kurstaki* (Btk) — biological

A naturally-occurring soil bacterium that produces Cry1 and Cry2
endotoxins selectively activated in the alkaline midguts of
Lepidoptera larvae. Highly selective: zero effect on non-target
arthropods, mammals, or pollinators.

## Use on corn for *H. zea* control

- Most effective on **L1–L2 larvae <½ inch** — older larvae stop
  feeding too soon to receive a lethal dose
- Apply in the **evening** to mitigate UV degradation; reapply after
  rain >½ inch
- Compatible in tank with most surfactants; avoid copper-based
  fungicides

## When to choose Btk over chemistry

- High beneficial-arthropod populations visible in scouting photos
- Organic-certified operation
- Field within Bt-corn refuge area where chemical pressure on
  *H. zea* would undermine resistance management
- Late-season pre-harvest interval concerns (no PHI on Btk)

## Limitations

- Short residual (3–5 days)
- Ineffective on L3+ larvae
- Will not rescue an already-advanced infestation; pair with timing
  scouting on egg lay
