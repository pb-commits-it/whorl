# Spinosad — IRAC 5 (spinosyns)

Spinosad is a fermentation-derived natural-product insecticide active on
Lepidoptera, Thysanoptera (thrips), and Diptera (leaf-mining flies). It
binds at a unique nicotinic-acetylcholine-receptor allosteric site
(Site I) `[irac-moa-v11.1]` — distinct from neonicotinoids (IRAC 4A)
and pyrethroids (IRAC 3A), making it ideal for rotation away from
either of those groups.

## Use on corn for *H. zea* control

- Most effective on **L1–L3 larvae**; efficacy drops sharply on L4+
- Apply when egg hatch is detected on silks; tank-mix only with
  compatible adjuvants (avoid alkaline carriers)
- **REI 4 h, PHI 1 d** for sweet corn (field corn labels typically match)

## Rotation pairs

Rotate generation-to-generation with [[moa/irac-28-diamides]] or
[[moa/irac-3a-pyrethroids]] (only where pyrethroid susceptibility is
maintained). **Never** follow spinosad with spinetoram in the same
generation — both are IRAC 5 and share cross-resistance risk.

## Beneficial-arthropod impact

Selective: low impact on parasitoid wasps, lady beetles, and lacewings
when applied per label. Preferred over broad-spectrum [[moa/irac-3a-pyrethroids]]
when beneficials are visibly present in scouting photos.
