# Chlorantraniliprole — IRAC 28 (diamides)

A ryanodine-receptor modulator — binds the calcium-release channel in
insect muscle, causing rapid feeding cessation followed by paralysis
and death `[irac-moa-v11.1]`. Best-in-class residual control of
Lepidoptera (corn earworm, fall armyworm, European corn borer) on
field corn.

## Use on corn for *H. zea* control

- Apply at **early egg lay through L2** for best results — ovicidal +
  larvicidal activity at label rates
- Residual control typically 14–21 days under Kansas summer
  conditions; longer with low-rainfall stretches
- **REI 4 h, PHI 1 d** for field corn

## Rotation pairs

Excellent rotation partner with [[moa/irac-5-spinosyns]] (different
target site, no cross-resistance) and [[moa/irac-3a-pyrethroids]]
where 3A susceptibility is maintained. **Avoid back-to-back diamide
applications** within a single pest generation to slow resistance
evolution.

## Beneficial-arthropod impact

Highly selective. Preferred when beneficials are visible in the scout
photo or when scouting later in the season (mid-July onward) when
natural enemy populations peak.
