# Bifenthrin — IRAC 3A (pyrethroids)

A type-I pyrethroid acting on voltage-gated sodium channels in insect
nerves `[irac-moa-v11.1]`. Cheap, fast-acting, and broadly effective
against many Lepidoptera and Coleoptera — but with significant
beneficial-arthropod toxicity and an established resistance footprint
across the Southern Plains.

## Use on corn for *H. zea* control

- **Caveat**: pyrethroid tolerance documented across the Southern
  Plains in *H. zea* populations. **Do not apply** to a field if an
  IRAC 3A spray has already been made in the current pest generation.
- Where used: best on L1–L2 larvae before silking; degrades quickly
  under high UV.
- **REI 12 h, PHI 1 d** for field corn.

## Rotation pairs

When 3A is the chosen current application, the *next* application
**must** be from a different MOA — [[moa/irac-5-spinosyns]] or
[[moa/irac-28-diamides]] preferred.

## Beneficial-arthropod impact

Broad-spectrum and non-selective. Will collapse local lady-beetle and
lacewing populations and can cause **spider-mite flaring** later in
the season. Prefer [[products/spinosad]] or
[[products/chlorantraniliprole]] when beneficials are visible in the
scout photo.
